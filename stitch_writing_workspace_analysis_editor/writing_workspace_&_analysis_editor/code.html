Chapter 1: The Inciting Incident

The rain in Neo-Veridia didn't wash things clean; it just made the grime slicker. Elara pulled her collar up against the acidic drizzle, the neon reflections of the advertising blimps dancing across her visor like ghosts trapped in a machine.

She hadn't planned on visiting the lower levels tonight. The data packet burned a virtual hole in her neural drive, encryption keys cycling uselessly against the firewall. She walked quickly down the alleyway, her boots splashing through puddles of iridescent oil. Shadows lengthened, merging with the darkness between the flickering streetlamps.

"You're late," a voice grated from the gloom. A figure detached itself from the wall, a bulky silhouette against the wet brick.

Elara stopped, hand hovering near the pulse-emitter at her hip. "Traffic was murder," she said, voice steady despite the adrenaline spiking her biometrics. "Literally. Two drones down on Sector 4."

The figure stepped into the light. It was Kaelen. His cybernetic eye whirred softly as it focused on her, the red lens contracting like a dying star. "Did you bring it?"

"Depends," Elara countered, "on whether you have the credits." She tapped her temple, indicating the secure storage.

Kaelen laughed, a sound like grinding gears. "Always business with you, Vance. That's why you're still alive in this city. Most people let their hearts bleed out before their wallets do." He tossed a cred-chip. It spun through the rain, a silver coin in the downpour, before she snatched it from the air.

She slotted it into her wrist reader. Green light. Valid. "Transfer initiating," she murmured. The neural link engaged, a sudden rush of cold data flowing from her mind to the local mesh network.

Suddenly, the air pressure dropped. The hum of the city ceased, replaced by a high-pitched whine that set her teeth on edge. Kaelen's red eye widened. "Jammer!" he hissed, drawing a kinetic pistol.
