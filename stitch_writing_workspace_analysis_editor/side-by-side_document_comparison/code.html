The sky was grey and ominous. He walked quickly down the street, keeping his head low to avoid eye contact with the passersby.

The city felt empty, hollow even. It was as if the soul of the place had been sucked out by the relentless rain that had been falling for days.

John turned the corner and saw the old building. It stood like a sentinel, guarding secrets that no one wanted to know. He hesitated for a moment, his hand resting on the cold brick wall.

He wasn't sure if he was ready for this. The weight of his decision pressed heavily on his chest.

The door creaked open, revealing a darkness that seemed to swallow the faint light from the streetlamps. He took a deep breath and stepped inside.
